Limits

The pieces are estimates and are revised. The latest days are provisional and are replaced as the estimates are updated. The residual is the small part of the market yield the split leaves unassigned, a few basis points. Expected inflation moves slowly, so most daily action lands in the real pieces. Read the level of a term premium with the disagreement between estimates in mind, and its changes with more confidence.
