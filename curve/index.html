The rate called for swings more than the rate itself, because the rate moves only part of the way each quarter. The rules called for far more than the Federal Reserve delivered in 2017 to 2019, when unemployment ran below its noncyclical rate, and for deeply negative rates in 2020. Limits

Forwards carry a term premium, so the priced curve is a break-even, not a forecast. The futures path carries a small premium as well. The neutral rate behind any policy rule is not observed, and every rule inherits that uncertainty. A committee that reacts to something the rules do not see will depart from them. The rule numbers move less often than the priced path, which moves every day. The curve on the rule path ignores changes in the term premium.
