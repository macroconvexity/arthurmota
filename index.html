About

I am an economist and macro strategist covering global macroeconomics, monetary policy and financial markets, with a focus on the US, Europe and China. This site collects my working papers, interactive models, notes and teaching material.

The work spans monetary economics, sovereign debt, portfolio theory, asset pricing, econometrics and selected alternative markets.

Research lab

01Risk model

02Yield decomposition

03Yield curve
Each Treasury vertex from 2 to 30 years, month by month to December of next year, as priced in the curve, with the federal funds path in futures against the median of twelve forward-looking policy rules.		[open]
04FX monitor
Dollar, euro, yen, pound and real against a fair value from rates, risk and oil.		[open]
05Inflation

06R-star
The US neutral real rate by sixteen measures: Federal Reserve models, market forwards, surveys and the Banco Central do Brasil toolkit carried to the US. Weekly.		[open]
07S&P 500 fair value
Weekly dividend discount model on bottom-up consensus earnings and Treasury real yields: the premium the index implies, and scenarios built from data.		[open]
08Quant book
A simulated cross-asset systematic book: nine published strategies across risk premia, flows and calendar effects, with positions, trades, risk and a test of the evidence.		[open]
