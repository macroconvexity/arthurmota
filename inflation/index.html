How to read this page

This page forecasts the US Consumer Price Index: the next monthly release, and the path of headline and core inflation to the end of next year. It is refreshed every day as energy prices move, and on each release day when the official number comes in.

Headline and core. Headline is the whole index. Core leaves out food and energy, which swing the most and say the least about where inflation is settling.

Two ways to quote it. The monthly change is seasonally adjusted, which is how the market reads a print. The 12-month rate is computed on the unadjusted index, which is how the BLS publishes it.

Built on public data. The methodology, inputs and code behind this page are not published. Not investment advice.

Release calendar

Dates are those published by the BLS, which has scheduled releases through the November 2026 index so far. A month shows no date until the BLS schedules it; none is inferred. Releases are at 8:30 in the morning, Eastern time.
