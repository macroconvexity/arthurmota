How to play

  1. Each turn: play any cards you can afford and make one chess move. After the move you can still play cards with spare mana (not Pawn Rush, Extra Step, Double Leap, Borrowed Moves or Second Move), then press End turn.
  2. You gain 2 mana each turn on top of what you did not spend, up to 5. Spent mana is gone; saved mana carries over.
  3. You draw 1 card per turn. Hand limit is 7; discard extras at turn end.
  4. Drag a card from your hand onto the board to play it. A card that needs a target then asks you to tap it, and you confirm before any mana is spent. Tapping a card only shows it bigger.
  5. Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a destination.
  6. Ice means frozen, a gold ring means it cannot be captured, bricks block movement.
  7. In check at turn start? A card may still rescue you. Mate is judged after cards.
  8. Checkmate wins. You can resign from the top bar at any time.
  9. Hover or press and hold a card to read it. The Cards button lists all 24.
  10. Esc cancels the pending card or selection.
