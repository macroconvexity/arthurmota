About

Macro research with a market lens.

This site is a public archive of independent research, quantitative tools, papers, notes and longer-form projects.

I am a macroeconomist and strategist working at the intersection of macroeconomic analysis, financial markets and quantitative research. My work focuses on turning macroeconomic questions into frameworks that can be tested, modelled and used in market decisions.

Research approach

The common thread across the work on this site is applied modelling. Topics range from monetary policy, inflation and sovereign debt to portfolio construction, derivatives, market microstructure and alternative asset pricing.

Research Lab

The Research Lab contains interactive models and prototypes built to explore economic scenarios, asset valuation, portfolio decisions and trading structures. These tools are separate from the formal research papers but often come from the same questions.
